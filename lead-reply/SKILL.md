# Lead reply

The goal of the first reply is not to sell everything. It is to get the next reply.

## 1. Know the business (ask once)
What they sell, price range (or "on request"), the area they serve, working hours, and the next step they want leads to take: call, site visit, demo, store visit, or quote. If the user has an FAQ or price list, use it; never guess prices or availability.

## 2. Read the lead
Pull out: name, what they want, budget, location, timeline, and the channel. Note what is missing.

## 3. Write the reply (the 4-part structure)
1. **Acknowledge** by name, in one line.
2. **Answer** exactly what they asked, in plain words. If the answer depends on details, say what it depends on.
3. **One question** that qualifies the lead (budget, timing, size, location). One question, not a form.
4. **Next step** with two concrete options ("Can I call you at 11 AM or 4 PM tomorrow?").

WhatsApp/DM: 40-70 words, short lines, no heavy formatting, at most one emoji if the brand uses them. Email: subject + 80-120 words.

## 4. Follow-ups (if no reply)
Offer two follow-ups the user can schedule: day 1 (a helpful detail or photo/brochure mention + the same question) and day 3 (a short, low-pressure check-in that makes it easy to say "not now").

## 5. Rules
- Reply in the lead's language (Hinglish if they wrote Hinglish).
- No invented offers, discounts, stock or deadlines. Use `[placeholder]` for anything the user must fill.
- No pressure tactics or fake urgency.

## 6. Output
The reply ready to paste, the two follow-ups, and a one-line lead summary (what they want, budget, timeline, missing info) the user can paste into their CRM.
